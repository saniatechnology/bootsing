# Barcelona Cultural Calendar — Aug 28 to Sep 27, 2026

A curated, category-spanning look at what's on in Barcelona over the next 30 days — leaning independent, artist-run, and discovery-oriented, with a few polished/institutional and "chic" picks mixed in. Compiled from web research on Aug 28, 2026; several smaller venues (queer collectives, artist-run factories, modular-synth meetups) publish programming on Instagram rather than indexable sites, so treat those entries as directional and worth a direct social-media check closer to the date. Links point to the venue/organizer's official page or the best available source for further details — for recurring club nights, the link goes to the venue's general schedule page rather than a single-event page.

**Legend:** 🔴 Last chance / closing soon · ⭐ One-off or rare · 🎉 Season finale/highlight
